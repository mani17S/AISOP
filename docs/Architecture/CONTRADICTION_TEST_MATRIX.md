## Corrections recorded while building this matrix
* **A test of mine was vacuous and has been fixed.** `test_stage6_security.py` test 9 and the first draft of S13 mutated a frozen `LLMResponse` (`resp.content = ...`), which raised a `ValidationError` and forced the workflow into its deterministic fallback; they never reached the claim validator. With a correct mutation (`model_copy(update=...)`) the real behaviour is: workflow status `validated`, guardrail decision `remediated`, a claim citing an analyst note is `unsupported` with its supporting ids stripped (`SEC-GR-TIER1-REF-MISSING`); a claim whose text conflicts with its cited event is demoted or marked `contradicted`. The earlier text "observed: ValidationError, reject_to_fallback" in `Stage6_Test_and_Validation_Addendum.md` and in the verification addendum was wrong about the mechanism (the conclusion that nothing is presented as validated holds). A "GAP-4: the validator checks references, not text" entry I drafted was wrong and was removed.
* S6b and the earlier `test_contradiction_model_spec.py` GAP-3 fixture used two different source systems for one native id; native ids are per-source namespaces, so both fixtures were changed to one source system, matching the design (CONTRADICTION_AWARE_SUFFICIENCY_DESIGN.md section 3).
